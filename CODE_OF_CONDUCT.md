# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. Its full text, at that link, applies to everyone who takes part: in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any other project space, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the work.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, and publishing someone's private information (including their account, tenant, or subscription details) aren't tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@MarcBushong](https://github.com/MarcBushong), through GitHub, and ask for a private channel. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's privacy is respected. If the report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature instead.

## Enforcement

The maintainer follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban, depending on the impact. Comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that break this code may be edited or removed.
